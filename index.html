Why Your Website Isn’t Capturing Leads

1

Your IDX Feed Makes You Invisible

Google can’t index your listing, because your IDX is hiding property data. So, buyers can’t find you and go to your competitor’s website instead.

2

You’re Missing What People Search for

People search for specific areas, schools, and communities before they search for an agent. Missing neighborhood-specific pages causes losing traffic on high-intent local searches.

3

You Have No Instant Home Valuation Tool

Home valuation tool turns curious homeowners into motivated seller leads. Websites without property-focused valuation tools leave opportunities on the table.

4

Your Bio & Website Feels Generic

If your website looks like every other agent’s, it’ll fail to build credibility and convince the prospect to choose you over your competitors.

5

You Listings Don’t Provide Enough Context

Families consider schools and neighborhoods before they visit. Without the local context they need to compare the property, your listings become less valuable.

6

Your Lead/Contact Form Is Hard to Find

If visitors need to hunt for the contact form, they’ll bounce without reaching out. Instead of turning visitors into qualified inquiries, your website loses the traffic too.

7

You Have No Content Strategy

Conversion requires trust and property listing alone isn’t enough. Your website needs helpful local guides, buying resources, market insights, and educational content.

8

You Don’t Care About Mobile Experience

Most buyers browse homes on their phones. If your website is slow or difficult to use on mobile, it’ll frustrate them enough to leave your site for good.

Built Specifically for Real Estate

Pixxen offers purpose-built websites for real estate agents, packed with features that attract more leads.

SEO-Indexable IDX Listings

Give every listing its own URL, schema markup, and indexable description so Google can read and rank them. When your individual listings rank, you attract more organic buyers.

Home Valuation Lead Magnet On Key Pages

Give homeowners another reason to contact you: place the “what-is-my-home-worth” tool right on your homepage and seller pages. Leverage instant value estimates to turn seller interest into qualified leads.

Saved Search + Property Alerts

Keep your visitors coming back with saved searches. Send automatic alerts whenever matching listings hit MLS. Keep your brand at the top of their minds throughout their home search.

Dedicated Neighborhood Pages with School Data

Help buyers compare localities, schools, and tax rates with dedicated neighborhood pages. Details like average pricing, GreatSchools ratings, and days on market help increase visibility for highly targeted local searches.

Interactive Map Search

Deliver a natural browsing experience with drag-to-search maps: the most engaging feature a buyer uses. Add school zone overlays, saved areas, and boundary-free exploration to keep your buyers connected.

Easy Mortgage Calculator + Affordability Tool

Help users estimate monthly payments without leaving your site. Add live monthly payment calculators on your listing pages along with adjustable down payment, taxes, or interest rates to keep visitors engaged.

Showcase Your Own Featured Listings

Keep the MLS feed separated from your own listings and highlight them with a hero carousel. Attract attention and get more showing requests through premium properties, 3D tours, or branded videos.

Behavior-triggered Lead Capture

Capture interested buyers at the right moment with prompts that appear at high-intent moments. Leverage behavior-based triggers such as repeat listing views, returning to the website, or saving a property.

Showcase Live Reviews from Authority Sites

Highlight verified reviews from trusted platforms to build credibility. Add a star schema so your ratings are visible in Google’s search results. Showcase your trust signals where conversion happens.

FAQs

Q Is IDX integration really included at $999?

Yes, it’s part of our real estate web design package. We’ll connect your IDX provider, configure property search, and ensure that your website can showcase your listings properly. If your MLS requires a specific IDX solution, we’ll help you choose the right one before development begins without any additional fee. However, third-party IDX subscription fee (if any) will be excluded.

Q Will my site rank for homes for sale in my city?

No agency can guarantee that. We’ll build your website with strong SEO fundamentals so you can compete in your local market. They include technical optimization, hyper-localized content, mobile-first design, and search-friendly structure.

Q How do you balance brokerage compliance and branding?

Before we launch your website, we’ll review it thoroughly to ensure it aligns with your brokerage’s guidelines, including branding requirements, IDX compliance rules, logo usage, equal housing disclosures, and MLS regulations. We’ll also make sure that your personal brand has enough room to grow and stand out.

Q How does this compare to the big IDX platform subscriptions?

Instead of renting a platform, you’re building a long-term marketing asset that grows with your business. Big IDX platforms usually require a hefty monthly fee and limit your flexibility. With us, you get a 100% customizable website. You own the website, content, and the domain. This gives you a much stronger brand positioning and increases lead quality.

Q Can you integrate multiple MLS feeds?

Yes, we can integrate multiple IDX feeds where it’s supported by your IDX provider and MLS agreements. Since multi-MLS setups vary in complexity and cost by region, we’ll review your coverage and recommend the best setup for your business in the kickoff call.

Q Can I add my own listings as featured properties?

Absolutely. Your homepage and key landing pages will highlight your featured listings independently of your standard IDX feed. This will give you more control over visibility as you’ll display your newest or highest-value listings above the general feed.

Q What about sold listings if my MLS doesn’t include them?

Some MLS systems don’t allow sold listings to be displayed through IDX. In that case, we can build you a separate sold portfolio that you maintain manually. This’ll double as proof of your production, and we’ll make sure that your website complies with your MLS and brokerage policies.

Q What happens after the 30-day support window?

You’ll continue to maintain and grow your website with full ownership over your domain, content, and files. During the support period, we’ll train your team on how to manage and customize your site. After 30 days, you can maintain it yourself, hire someone, or come back to us for further support.

Q Will you provide SEO content after the launch?

The package includes the SEO foundation you need to launch your website successfully. If you need help, ongoing SEO is available as a separate service. You don’t need additional SEO support for the launch as part of this real estate website design package.

Q Will I own the website?

Of course. You’ll have complete control over the access and maintenance of your website. This includes ownership over the website, domain, branding, content, design, and source files. There are absolutely no contracts or restrictions that prevent you from moving your website to another hosting provider or developer.
